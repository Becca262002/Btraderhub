📖 AI Scanner Strategy Guide

🧠 How the AI Scanner Works

The AI Scanner analyses real Deriv tick data (last 1000 ticks per market) and calculates probabilities for each trade type. It uses Bollinger Bands, RSI, digit frequency analysis and price momentum to generate signals.

📊 OVER/UNDER STRATEGIES

Over 1,2,3 Strategy:

  • Digits 0,1,2,3 should all be below 10% (red/yellow bars among them)
  • At least 2 of digits 4-9 should be above 11% (green/blue bars)
  • Entry: Wait for the least appearing digit among 1,2,3 to appear → next tick enters if digit 4-9 appears

Under 8,7,6 Strategy:

  • Digits 6,7,8,9 should all be below 10% (red/yellow bars among them)
  • At least 2 of digits 0-5 should be above 11% (green/blue bars)
  • Entry: Wait for the least appearing among 6,7,8 → next tick enter if digit 0-4 appears

🔵 EVEN/ODD STRATEGIES

Odd Strategy:

  • 🟢 Green bar + 🔵 Blue bar both on ODD digits (1,3,5,7,9) at 11%+
  • 🔴 Red bar ≤ 8.6% + 🟡 Yellow bar ≤ 9.5% both on EVEN digits
  • Entry: Wait for least even digit (R/Y) to appear → then 2 consecutive odds in next 5 ticks → enter
  • ⚠️ Stop after 3-7 wins and re-check market conditions

Even Strategy:

  • 🟢 Green bar + 🔵 Blue bar both on EVEN digits (0,2,4,6,8) at 11%+
  • 🔴 Red bar ≤ 8.6% + 🟡 Yellow bar ≤ 9.5%
  • Entry: Wait for odd digit among R/Y to appear → even digit within next 3 ticks → enter
  • ⚠️ Stop after 3-7 wins and re-check market conditions

📈 ONLY UPS / ONLY DOWNS (BB + RSI)

  • Only Ups: RSI 48-68 + price above BB middle + bands expanding + 60%+ upward momentum → 2-3 ticks
  • Only Downs: RSI 32-52 + price below BB middle + bands expanding + 60%+ downward momentum → 2-3 ticks

🎨 BAR COLOR KEY

GREEN = Most appearing digit
BLUE = 2nd most appearing
YELLOW = 2nd least appearing
RED = Least appearing digit

⚠️ DIFFERS & MATCHES (Coming from AI)

Differs [Red Bar]: Trade when the last digit DIFFERS from your prediction. Best when red bar digit is consistently appearing — fade it by choosing Differs.

Matches [Green Bar]: Trade when the last digit MATCHES your prediction. Best when green bar digit is appearing 14%+ (well above expected 10%) — ride the hot digit.
